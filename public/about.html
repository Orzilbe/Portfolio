About Me

My professional expertise lies in bridging the gap between complex technical infrastructures and intuitive user experiences. When I'm not re-engineering workflows or exploring new game mechanics, you'll likely find me practicing Pilates or finding new ways to integrate technology into everyday life to improve efficiency and experience.
