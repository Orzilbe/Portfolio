About Me

Hi, I'm Or! I'm an Industrial Engineer (B.Sc.) from Ben-Gurion University, specializing in Information Systems and currently serving as a Product & System Implementation Manager.

My professional expertise lies in bridging the gap between complex technical infrastructures and intuitive user experiences. Whether I'm architecting end-to-end digital solutions on monday.com or optimizing large-scale operational workflows for the Ministry of Education, my focus is always on creating systems that are both powerful and user-centric.

I am currently pursuing my M.A. in Digital Game Design and Development at Shenkar. This journey allows me to merge engineering precision with game design principles—leveraging engagement loops and advanced UX to build the next generation of interactive software and AI-driven tools.

When I’m not re-engineering workflows or exploring new game mechanics, you’ll likely find me practicing Pilates or finding new ways to integrate technology into everyday life to improve efficiency and experience.
