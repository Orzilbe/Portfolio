Rhythm
Revenge

Overview

An immersive rhythm-based game inspired by Tap Tap Revenge, developed as part of the Sound Design course at Shenkar. The project explores the synchronization between visual feedback and complex audio patterns.

The Concept

The core focus was on creating a tight relationship between audio cues and player actions. Every "tap" is designed to feel as part of the soundtrack, enhancing the player's musical agency.

Sound Design

Focused on high-fidelity audio feedback. The soundscape was carefully crafted to guide the player through increasing levels of BPM and rhythmic complexity.
