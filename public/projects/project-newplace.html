Interactive Narrative Game

A Place for Someone New

Overview

A Hebrew-language narrative management sim that puts you in the shoes of a new mother. Navigate the emotional and logistical chaos of preparing for a newborn - on a tight budget, under family pressure, and with no instruction manual.

Concept

The game unfolds in two acts. In Act 1, you furnish a baby's room within a ₪4,000 budget - shopping, negotiating, and deciding whether to heed your mother's superstitions about bringing items in before birth. In Act 2, you manage a newborn's first day through stealth-based lullaby mechanics, task tracking, and real-time sanity management.

Technical Stack

Built with vanilla JavaScript and HTML5. An AI-powered WhatsApp-style interface, driven by the Groq API, generates dynamic conversations with family members, vendors, and friends - making every playthrough feel alive and unpredictable.
