Rush Hour
Extreme

An Upgraded Puzzle Experience

Overview

An expanded redesign of the classic Rush Hour sliding puzzle. The original game gets a full upgrade with 10 handcrafted levels and four special vehicle types, each with unique abilities that fundamentally change how the puzzle is solved.

The Upgrade

The core challenge was layering new mechanics on top of a familiar ruleset without breaking the game's balance. Each special vehicle introduces a distinct decision point - do you save your ambulance jump for later, or use the police car's freedom now?

Special Vehicles

🚑
Ambulance
Jumps over blocking cars
🚔
Police Car
Moves in any direction
🚚
Tow Truck

⚠️

Built With

React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · Drag-based interaction system with pixel-accurate grid collision detection.
